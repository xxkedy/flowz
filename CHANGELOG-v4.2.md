# Flowz v4.2 — Profile Sync + TOEIC Study

Released: 2026-08-06

## Sync
- Moves Duo Sync to the bottom of the page because it is primarily a one-time setup feature.
- Collapses a connected Duo room into a compact status strip; tap it to inspect settings.
- Uses the currently selected kedy or Leni profile as the local sync target instead of continuing to display the browser's original setup role.
- Keeps local History Vault protection active while the selected profile is synced.

## Learning modes
- COMMUTE remains the daily speaking and CEFR session.
- TOEIC CHECK remains a scored 12-question Listening and Reading mini-check.
- BATH is repurposed as TOEIC STUDY: a 15-minute screen-and-audio teaching session designed for bath time.
- mikan remains the dedicated vocabulary routine; Flowz TOEIC STUDY uses vocabulary inside questions, conversations, and passages rather than duplicating flashcards.
- FREE remains practical conversation.

## TOEIC estimate
- Adds local storage for TOEIC Check results.
- After a TOEIC Check, Flowz asks for Listening /6 and Reading /6 and displays a broad, explicitly unofficial estimated score band.
- One result is LOW confidence; the latest three results produce a narrower MEDIUM-confidence trend.
