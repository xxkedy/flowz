# Flowz v3.7 — Conversation Variety

Released: 2026-08-06

## Changed
- Added a short Arrival Review when kedy is nearly at work or home: three used phrases, up to two natural corrections, and one phrase to reuse next time.
- Reuses one or two phrases from the most recent Notion Diary English Log when available.
- Prevents repetitive default questions about after-work plans, dinner, and weekends unless kedy introduces them.
- Rotates conversation across commute observations, concrete work situations, music production, health, apps/AI, money, travel, memories, opinions, and simple hypothetical choices.
- When kedy asks to stay in chat, repetition stops immediately and shadowing is not suggested again during that session.
- Removed end-or-continue prompts; the session ends only on `まとめて` or `Wrap up`.

## Unchanged
- Hidden mission flow and 80% conversation / 20% shadowing balance.
- One-retry correction rule.
- Existing Diary append-and-verify workflow.
- Ordinary learning sessions do not update GitHub.
