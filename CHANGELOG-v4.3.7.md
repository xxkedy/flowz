# Flowz v4.3.7

Date: 2026-08-06

## TOEIC Study Voice Talk optimization

- Changed TOEIC Study from a 15-minute mixed screen/audio session to exactly 5 questions in about 5–8 minutes.
- Optimized every item for Voice Talk, where previous text disappears when kedy starts speaking.
- Removed tasks that require reading while speaking, reciting visible text, long passages, and Part 7 reading.
- Fixed the daily mix to 2 listening questions, 1 Part 2-style response, and 2 short grammar or sentence-completion questions.
- Kept mikan as the separate 30-question vocabulary routine.
- Added automatic wrap-up after question 5 instead of repeatedly asking kedy to say Wrap up.
- Preserved short Japanese explanations, reusable TOEIC patterns, weak-area prioritization from Notion, and verified Diary logging.

## UI

- TOEIC Study label: `Voice 5Q · 5–8min`
- TOEIC Study icon: `🛁🎤`
- Mission guide now explains that the mode is audio-first and does not require looking at the screen while answering.
