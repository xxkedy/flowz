# Flowz v4.8.5 — COMMUTE Conversation Continuity

Date: 2026-08-21

## Changed
- COMMUTE no longer treats short praise/backchannels as a natural stopping point.
- The coach is instructed to keep the conversational ball moving when kedy gives short or hesitant replies.
- Shadowing is now explicitly a brief insert inside the conversation; after one repetition the coach returns to the prior topic instead of ending or going silent.
- Useful phrase pickup is encouraged in-context, with short human-style prompts rather than lesson-mode drills.
- Question chains are reduced by mixing reactions, opinions, observations, topic expansion, and occasional questions.
- When kedy asks the coach to lead, the coach takes more responsibility for topic flow while keeping spoken English short and audio-friendly.

## Scope
- kedy COMMUTE only.
- TOEIC, FREE, Leni modes, Duo Sync, XP, and storage behavior are unchanged.
